Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Zero Data Collection

Balero-S2 does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, or identifying information. The application runs completely offline on your device.

Files & Permissions

Balero-S2 accesses only the files and folders explicitly chosen by the user through the iOS document picker or stored inside the application's local Documents container.

Third-Party Services

Balero-S2 does not include any third-party SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking networks.
